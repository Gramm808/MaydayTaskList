Steam Line System Safety

Read this before working on or near any steam line.

Steam line safety

  1. 1.The steam is roughly 300°F and is hot enough to cause instant burns. Be extremely careful around all steam lines and fittings.
  2. 2.Close the main steam supply valve to the line in the boiler room whenever you are not actively testing steam lines. Always close it before leaving the facility.
  3. 3.If you need to open a steam line after it has been supplied with steam to make a repair, make sure the section you need to work on is isolated and any residual pressure is relieved from the system.
  4. 4.Make sure there is no plastic of any kind in contact with steam or condensate lines. If you find any, push a piece of wood in between them to create immediate temporary separation.
