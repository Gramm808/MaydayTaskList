Drilling and Tapping Holes in the Plastic Tanks

Notes for drilling and tapping holes into the top of the plastic tanks.

Drilling and tapping notes

  1. 1.The tan material is a very soft plastic and does not tap easily.
  2. 2.Drill the hole about 1-1/2 inches deep. You will hit steel at this point. Stop drilling.
  3. 3.Do not tap the hole in one pass. The soft plastic heats up quickly and begins to squeeze the tap.
  4. 4.Tap the hole in 1/2 inch increments. Back the tap out and clean the plastic off between each increment.
  5. 5.Do not over-torque the bolt. Turn the bolt until the head makes contact, then an additional 1/4 turn.
