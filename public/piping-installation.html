Piping Installation Workmanship Expectations

Review these expectations before starting work. Raise questions or needed exceptions with the project lead before installing.

1. Piping layout

  1. 1.1.Install horizontal piping level and vertical piping plumb, unless the system requires a specified slope or another approved arrangement.
  2. 1.2.Keep piping straight and related runs parallel, with consistent spacing.
  3. 1.3.Confirm required slopes and routing changes before installation. Do not create unintended low points or reverse slopes.

2. Alignment and supports

  1. 2.1.Align piping naturally with valves, pumps, equipment, and other connections.
  2. 2.2.Do not use flange bolts or other connections to pull misaligned piping into position.
  3. 2.3.Connected piping must not place unintended twisting, bending, or strain on components.
  4. 2.4.Support piping properly. Do not let pumps, valves, or equipment connections carry the piping’s weight unless designed for it.

3. Weld quality

  1. 3.1.Use the welding procedure and rod or filler specified for the piping material and service.
  2. 3.2.Prepare and clean joints properly before welding.
  3. 3.3.Welds must meet the specified acceptance criteria. Use approved pass/fail photos or sample welds as a visual reference. See Weld Quality Reference.
  4. 3.4.Remove slag and loose spatter so welds can be inspected. Do not paint or cover welds before required inspection.
  5. 3.5.Correct rejected welds before testing or placing the piping in service.

4. Maintenance and repair access

  1. 4.1.Install serviceable components so they can be removed without cutting pipe or welds, or rebuilt in place according to the manufacturer’s instructions.
  2. 4.2.Provide unions, flanges, or removable pipe sections where needed for disassembly.
  3. 4.3.Leave enough clearance to remove bolts, covers, strainers, and internal parts using normal tools.
  4. 4.4.Keep valve handles and maintenance access points accessible.

5. Review before completion

  1. 5.1.Check layout, alignment, supports, welds, and maintenance access before calling the work complete.
  2. 5.2.Complete required inspections and leak or pressure tests before startup.
  3. 5.3.Correct deficiencies and review any approved exceptions with the project lead.
